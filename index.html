FAQ

Who needs AI governance?

Any organization deploying AI at scale — especially in regulated industries (finance, healthcare, public sector). The EU AI Act mandates it; NIST recommends it; customers expect it.

Can you audit existing AI systems?

Yes. Discovery starts with an inventory of all AI/ML systems, classifies each by risk level, and produces a remediation roadmap.

Do you support sector-specific frameworks?

Yes — OCC/Fed guidance for financial services, HIPAA/SaMD for healthcare, and emerging standards for education and government.
